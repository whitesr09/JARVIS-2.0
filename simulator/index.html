Updates & Architecture

How Jarvis works — and what keeps it safe

Verified actions & recovery

Every tap is verified: Jarvis observes the screen, checks the result of each action, and stops safely when the UI is unclear instead of guessing. Failed steps are retried once, then reported — never forced.

Floating avatar

The companion avatar floats over any app while Jarvis works — always-on or working-only, draggable with edge docking. Single-tap toggles listening or pauses the task; double-tap opens the task progress HUD. Try it: pick an avatar in Settings → Overlay Avatars.

Task Progress HUD

A compact 4-step status card shows the goal, recent steps, a running indicator and completed checks — so you always know what Jarvis is doing inside other apps. Run the demo task on Home to see it live.

Silent Execution

Silent Execution mutes task narration while keeping normal conversation audible. Progress stays visible in the HUD, and a subtle chime still plays when a task finishes.

Safety guardrails

Confirmation appears only for hard guardrails: payments, delete-for-everyone, calls to numbers outside your contacts, ambiguous message recipients, and bulk actions (hard cap per run, stop if the list stops moving). Everything else executes and verifies. Emergency stop: tap the avatar, touch the screen, or say “Jarvis, stop”.

Zero-backend privacy

No accounts, no sign-up, no external database, no tracking. Voice biometrics and memories live on the device; the connection runs directly from your phone to your AI provider over TLS.
